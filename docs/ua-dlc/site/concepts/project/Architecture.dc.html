All Decisions
  1. D-1

    14 sourcing countries for FW27

    They cover 93% of FW27 FOB spend, so volumes are read for those countries only.

    RARina Alvarez, Oct 14

  2. D-2

    Month-end FX rates from Treasury

    Finance closes on them, so scenarios match the close. Tariff models.xlsx is Confidential and was not indexed.

    DODana Ortiz, Oct 15

  3. D-3

    Tariff rates from the nightly HTS sync

    It is the source Finance already reconciles against; customs broker sheets lag by weeks.

    RALRRina Alvarez, Luis Romero, Oct 16

  4. D-4

    Saved scenarios stay in the app

    A team prototype does not write back to SAP. Scenarios live in the app’s own table in the team-apps environment and expire with it.

    OHOmar Haddad, Oct 20
